The Ashford Estate — Terrace, 9:52 PM

Rain has just stopped. Lanterns swing over the terrace, casting long shadows across the stone. The victim — Lady Ashford — was found beneath the eastern railing. No forced entry. No strangers. Four guests remain, and every one of them is lying about something.

Your Task

Collect all eight pieces of evidence, interview all four suspects, then solve three puzzles to unlock the final accusation.

How to Play

Click any tab to explore. Evidence and suspect interviews are saved automatically in your browser.

Warning

The Evidence Board

The Three Investigations

Each puzzle unlocks after all evidence is collected and every suspect interviewed.

Puzzle I — Reconstruct the Timeline

Arrange the eight pieces of evidence in their true chronological order. One item predates the party; the rest occurred the night of the murder.

Puzzle II — Expose the Contradictions

Pair each suspect with the single piece of evidence that most directly contradicts their alibi (or confirms their motive).

Puzzle III — Motive & Opportunity

Only one suspect possessed both a clear motive and the confirmed opportunity at 9:52 PM. Select them.
